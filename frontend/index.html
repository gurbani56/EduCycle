How it works

Three steps from shelf to classmate

Step 1 · List

List what you can spare

Describe the item, choose whether to donate, lend or exchange it, and pick an approved pickup point on campus.

Step 2 · Connect

Request what you need

Send a request with a short message. The owner accepts or declines, and you are notified either way.

Step 3 · Exchange

Meet at the pickup point

Hand the item over at the agreed point and confirm it on EduCycle. Loans are completed when the item is returned.

Safe exchanges

Every exchange happens at a known place

Students can only arrange pickups at locations reviewed by an EduCycle administrator. You can suggest a new spot, and it becomes available once approved.

Read the safety guidelines See pickup points
  • Approved pickup points only. Listings with an unreviewed location cannot be requested.
  • Signed-in students. You need an account to request, list or contact anyone.
  • Report a problem. Grievances go to the admin team, and you can follow their status.
