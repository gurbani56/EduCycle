Terms of use

The rules every EduCycle user agrees to. They keep the platform fair and useful for everyone.

1. Using EduCycle

EduCycle is for students sharing academic resources. You must provide accurate account details and keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account.

2. Listings

  • List only items you own and are allowed to give away, lend or exchange.
  • Describe the item and its condition honestly.
  • Do not list anything unsafe, illegal, counterfeit, recalled or unrelated to study.
  • EduCycle is not for paid sales. Do not ask for money.

3. Exchanges

  • Arrange exchanges only at approved pickup points.
  • Keep to what you agreed. If plans change, cancel or decline in EduCycle so the other student is informed.
  • Borrowers must return lent items on time and in the same condition.
  • EduCycle is not a party to exchanges and cannot guarantee the condition of items.

4. Conduct

Be respectful. Harassment, discrimination, threats, spam and misuse of the grievance system are not allowed.

5. Moderation

Administrators may disable listings or accounts that break these terms, and record the reason. You can respond through a grievance. Administrators may also reject suggested pickup points that are unsuitable.

6. Changes

These terms may be updated. Continued use after an update means you accept the new terms.
