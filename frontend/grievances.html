Report a problem

Tell the EduCycle administrators about a problem with a listing, an exchange, a pickup point, another student or the website. Only you and the administrators can see what you submit.

EduCycle is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact campus security or your local emergency number first. You can report the incident here afterwards.

New grievance

Safety concerns are marked high priority for the administrators.

What happens next

  1. You get a reference number (for example GRV-2026-ABC123). Keep it for your records.
  2. Your grievance starts as Open. An administrator reviews it and may change it to In review.
  3. The administrator writes a response and marks it Resolved, or Closed – not upheld with an explanation.
  4. You get a notification whenever the status or response changes.
