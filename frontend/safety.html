Safety guidelines

Simple practices that keep exchanges smooth and safe for everyone.

EduCycle is not an emergency service. If you feel unsafe or are in danger, contact campus security or your local emergency number straight away.

Meet at approved pickup points

  • Only arrange exchanges at the approved point shown on the request.
  • Choose busy times when other people are around.
  • Do not go to someone’s room or an isolated place to collect an item.
  • If a pickup point no longer feels suitable, report it under “Pickup/drop-off location issue”.

Protect your personal information

  • EduCycle never shows your email to other students. You decide what else to share.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes or bank details.
  • EduCycle does not involve payments. Be wary of anyone asking for money.

Check items before confirming

  • Inspect the item at the pickup point before you confirm you received it.
  • Listers should describe any defects honestly in the description.
  • Electrical items should be checked by a lab instructor before use.
  • Do not list anything dangerous, recalled, illegal or that you do not own.

Report problems

  • Use “Report it” on a listing that is misleading or inappropriate.
  • Use Report a problem for no-shows, misconduct or safety concerns. Safety concerns are marked high priority.
  • You can follow every grievance’s status and the administrator’s response.
