Privacy policy

What EduCycle stores, who can see it, and how long it is kept. This page describes how the software works; your campus deployment may add its own policy.

Information we store

  • Account details: your name, email address, department and semester. Your password is stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash and cannot be read by anyone.
  • Listings: the details you provide, your chosen pickup point and the listing’s status.
  • Requests and exchanges: who requested what, messages you attach, and each status change with its date.
  • Location suggestions and grievances: what you submit and the administrators’ decisions and responses.
  • Activity log and notifications: a record of important actions (for example, “listed an item”), used for your history and for moderation. Passwords and session tokens are never logged.
  • Session cookie: one strictly necessary cookie (educycle.sid) keeps you signed in. It is HTTP-only and is not used for tracking or advertising.

Who can see it

  • Other students see your name, your listings and, for exchanges you are part of, your messages and the agreed pickup point.
  • Your email address is visible only to you and the administrators.
  • Your grievances are visible only to you and the administrators.
  • Administrators can see account details and activity in order to moderate the platform. They cannot see your password.

How long we keep it

Records are kept while your account exists so exchange histories stay accurate for both students. Deleted listings are hidden but kept in exchange history. To have your account and personal data removed, submit a request under “Website or technical issue”.

Third parties

EduCycle does not sell or share your data and does not use third-party analytics or advertising. Fonts and interface libraries are served from the EduCycle server itself.
