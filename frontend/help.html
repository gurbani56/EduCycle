Help and FAQ

Answers to common questions about listing, requesting and exchanging resources.

Getting started

Any student can create an account with their email address. You need an account to request, list or report anything. Browsing is open to everyone.

No. EduCycle does not charge fees and does not allow paid sales. Items are donated, lent or exchanged.

Academic resources such as textbooks, notes, calculators, electronics for projects, lab equipment and stationery. Do not list anything unsafe, illegal, or that you do not own.

Requests and exchanges

The owner is notified and can accept or decline. If they accept, the item is reserved for you and the request shows the agreed pickup point. Meet there, receive the item, and confirm it in Your activity.

After the owner hands the item over, its status shows as On loan. When you return it, the owner confirms the return and the listing becomes available again.

Yes. You can cancel while it is pending or accepted. If it was already accepted, the item becomes available to others again and the owner is notified.

A listing can only be requested when it is Available and its pickup point is approved. If the owner chose a location that is still waiting for review, the listing shows “Pickup point awaiting approval”.

Pickup points

A public place on campus where students meet to hand over items. Only points approved by an administrator can be used.

Go to Pickup points, or use “Suggest a new location” on the listing form. An administrator reviews it, and you are notified whether it was approved. If it is rejected, you can see the reason.

Problems and accounts

Use Report a problem in the account menu, or “Report it” on a listing. You get a reference number and can follow the status and the administrator’s response.

Automated password reset by email is not available yet. Contact the administrators as described on the Contact page and they can help you regain access.

Administrators can disable listings that break the terms of use. The reason is shown in My listings. If you disagree, submit a grievance and explain why.

Still need help?

If your question is not answered here, submit a grievance under “Website or technical issue” and an administrator will respond.
